Problem Statement

The Communication & Interaction Barrier

🔇 Sign Language Inaccessibility

Over 70 million deaf individuals globally use sign language as their primary mode of communication. The general public lacks fluency, creating everyday barriers in workplaces, hospitals, and public services.

  • High reliance on human interpreters.
  • Lack of accessible real-time translation tools.

🖱️ Hardware Limitations & Hygiene

Traditional Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) strictly relies on physical contact (keyboards, mice, touchscreen displays). In sterile settings (surgical theaters) or industrial plants, touchless control is essential.

  • Physical peripherals are prone to contamination.
  • Inconvenient during presentations or remote workflows.
Project Goals

Objectives of HandLingo

1 Real-Time Translation

Accurately capture 21 hand landmarks using a standard RGB camera and translate static & dynamic signs (A, B, C, D...) into sentences.

2 Speech Synthesis (TTS)

Bridge the acoustic gap by converting assembled gesture words directly into clear audio speech for non-signers.

3 Touchless OS Interaction

Provide zero-contact computer control: intuitive air-mouse, presentation slide switching, and system audio adjustment.

4 Digital Air Canvas

Allow touch-free finger air drawing and sketching for classrooms, meetings, and creative expressions.

5 Zero Cost & Accessible

Run on ubiquitous webcams without requiring expensive sensory gloves, depth sensors, or LiDAR hardware.

6 Cross-Platform Growth

A web-first platform architected for seamless evolution into mobile and edge-computing applications.

Proposed Solution

Unified Gesture AI Platform

💡 Core Innovation

HandLingo eliminates sensory gloves by leveraging single-camera Computer Vision and spatial Machine Learning algorithms.

  • Two-Way Communication: Signs translated to visual text and spoken speech instantly.
  • Multi-Modal Controller: Seamlessly switch between Sign Translator, Presentation Mode, Volume Slider, and Air Drawing.
  • No Calibration Overhead: Instantaneous hand detection upon entering camera FOV.

🚀 Key Differentiators

  • Webcam-Only: Zero specialized sensor requirement.
  • Sub-30ms Latency: Optimized Euclidean coordinate tracking ensures real-time feedback.
  • Process Isolation: Modular Python feature threads prevent webcam contention and memory bottlenecks.
  • Intuitive UI: Interactive dashboard with one-click toggles and live status badges.

Workflow

Working Methodology

1. Landmark Extraction

Frames converted from BGR to RGB. MediaPipe extracts spatial coordinates of thumb tip (ID 4), index tip (ID 8), and wrist (ID 0).

2. Spatial Geometry Analysis

Euclidean distance formula calculates finger proximity:
d = √((x₂ - x₁)² + (y₂ - y₁)²) Detects pinch gestures, finger fold states, and hand angles.

3. Action Dispatching

Mapped commands trigger designated modules:
• Distance ∝ Volume %
• Index position → Canvas draw
• Swipe left/right → Slide change
• Alphabet sign → Text queue

System Capabilities

Major Features & Modules

🗣️ Sign Language to Speech

Real-time translation of finger spelling (A-Z) and standard signs. Assembles letters into words, words into sentences, and synthesizes clear vocal speech.

🔊 Smart Volume Controller

Thumb-to-index pinch distance dynamically scales master system volume from 0% to 100% with real-time HUD bar visualization.

🎨 Virtual Air Drawing

Finger tip tracking acts as a virtual stylus. Supports multi-color selection, variable brush strokes, and air-swipe canvas clearing.

📊 Presentation & Media Control

Hands-free navigation for slide decks (Next/Previous) and multimedia playback (Play, Pause, Skip) using directional hand sweeps.
