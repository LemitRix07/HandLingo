About HandLingo

AI-Powered Hand Gesture Communication & Interaction Platform

▣ PROJECT OVERVIEW

HandLingo is an AI-powered hand gesture communication and interaction platform designed to make digital communication and computer interaction more accessible, intuitive and touchless.

The system uses computer vision, hand tracking and machine learning to capture hand movements through a camera, detect hand landmarks and understand different gestures.

Its primary function is Sign Language Translation, where recognized signs such as A, B, C, D and other gestures can be converted into text, combined into sentences and converted into speech.

Beyond translation, HandLingo is designed as a multi-purpose gesture control platform that can interact with digital applications using hand movements.
